## What Is OSPL Grocery
OSPL Grocery is a complete ecommerce software solution developed by **Originate Soft Pvt Ltd**, a custom software and web development company based in Kolkata, India.
It is built specifically for the operational demands of grocery and FMCG retail — weighted items, real-time stock changes, delivery slot coordination, and short shelf-life inventory. Standard ecommerce platforms are not designed for these requirements. OSPL Grocery is.

The platform follows a **single-operator, dark-store model**: one business manages its own catalog, pricing, delivery zones, and fulfillment from a single back end. This is not a multi-vendor marketplace. It is your own storefront, fully under your control.

The software is **ready-made and customizable**. You get a working platform from day one. Every component — storefront, mobile apps, admin panel, delivery management — can be configured and extended to fit your specific business model.

**Built for:**
- Independent grocery stores and supermarket chains going online
- FMCG brands building a direct-to-customer sales channel
- Dark-store operators managing hyperlocal delivery zones
- Retailers running multiple branches from one back end

## How You Can Make Money

OSPL Grocery is a revenue-generating platform from day one. Here are the primary income streams:

**1. Direct Product Sales**
Sell your own grocery and FMCG inventory directly to customers. No commission to a third-party platform. Every sale goes to you.

**2. Delivery Charges**
Set delivery fees by zone, order value, or time slot. Free delivery thresholds can be configured to increase average order value.

**3. Premium and Express Slots**
Charge a premium for express delivery or peak-hour slots. Standard slots can remain free to drive adoption.

**4. Promotional Placements**
If you choose to onboard FMCG brand partners, you can charge for featured product placements, banner slots, or sponsored category positions on your storefront.

**5. Subscription Plans for Customers**
Offer a monthly or annual subscription for free delivery or exclusive discounts — a recurring revenue model that increases customer retention.

**6. Bulk and B2B Orders**
Configure a separate B2B ordering channel for restaurants, offices, and institutions buying in volume. Separate pricing tiers, bulk discounts, and invoice-based payment options are available.

## Installation Steps

OSPL Grocery is delivered as a fully installed and configured setup on your server. The following outlines the standard deployment process:

**Step 1 — Share Your Requirements**
Contact Originate Soft with your business details: store name, delivery zones, preferred payment gateways, and any customization requirements.

**Step 2 — Scope and Proposal**
Our team reviews your requirements and provides a detailed scope, timeline, and pricing proposal. Standard delivery is within 7–14 working days depending on customization scope.

**Step 3 — Server Setup**
You provide server credentials (AWS, GCP, DigitalOcean, or any VPS). Our team handles the environment setup: server configuration, domain mapping, SSL, and database initialization.

**Step 4 — Platform Configuration**
We configure your catalog structure, delivery zones, time slots, payment gateways, and admin credentials. Your branding — logo, colors, app name — is applied across all components.

**Step 5 — Testing and Handover**
End-to-end testing across storefront, admin panel, and mobile apps. A walkthrough session is conducted with your team. Full source code and documentation are handed over.

**Step 6 — Go Live**
Platform goes live. Post-launch support is available for bug fixes, updates, and feature additions.
